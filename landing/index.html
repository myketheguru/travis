Travis is built for desktop.

Travis is a native macOS, Windows, and Linux app — Cmd / Ctrl + J anywhere is its primary surface, and a phone can't run it. Open this page on your computer to install.

Capture at the speed of thought

Hit Cmd / Ctrl + J anywhere. Type what you're thinking. Travis turns it into structured tasks, reminders, and notes — and asks one good question if it needs to.

It actually does the work

Defer a task, draft an email for confirmation, log a reminder, run a quick check on your computer, copy something to your clipboard — Travis proposes the action, you tap once, it ships.

Proactive, not chatty

Quietly checks in a few times during waking hours — only when there's something specific worth surfacing. Stays silent otherwise. No notification spam.

Email + calendar, on your terms

Connect Gmail or Outlook. Travis drafts the message, you review and confirm, it sends. Reads your calendar so it knows what tomorrow looks like before it suggests anything.

Tailored to your work

Tell Travis what you do — what your org does, who you serve, what to pay attention to — and every reply gets grounded in your actual context. Not generic AI fluff.

Honest about its limits

When something's beyond Travis, it says so out loud and tracks the request. No silent failures. No fake confidence. You always know what just happened.

Local-first by design

Your notes, conversations, and credentials live on your machine. The LLM is the only thing that ever calls out — and you pick which one (Claude, OpenAI, or local Ollama).

Pick your own brain

Switch between Claude, OpenAI, or a fully-local Ollama model in Settings. Bring your own API key. No vendor lock-in.
