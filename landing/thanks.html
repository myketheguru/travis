Travis is a native macOS, Windows, and Linux app. Open this page on your computer to install — your phone can't run it.

First run — what to expect

What's next

  • Cmd / Ctrl + J opens Travis from anywhere. Type what you're thinking — it captures, structures, and acts.
  • Tell Travis a bit about your work in Settings → Context so its replies stay grounded in what you actually do.
  • Connect Gmail or Outlook in Settings → Connections so Travis can draft and send email on your behalf, with a confirmation card.
  • Updates ship automatically — Travis checks the release feed and installs new versions when you tap Settings → Updates.
